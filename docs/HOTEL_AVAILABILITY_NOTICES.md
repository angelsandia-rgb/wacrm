# Concise availability notices

When a room selection or captured details produce a new calculable total, include the verified estimate in the primary reply. Record the estimate send only after the primary send succeeds. The existing post-send estimate path remains a fallback, rather than sending a second copy seconds later.

Share the full authorized availability window once in a recent exchange: maximum two hours during reception's daytime hours, or reception hours 08:00–20:00 overnight. Later estimates keep a short subject-to-availability caveat. Factual follow-up answers do not repeat standalone hotel-confirmation boilerplate. A direct question about whether a booking is confirmed requires the explicit notice again; pending prices, payments, cancellations and actual availability decisions remain protected by their existing rules.

History checks use assistant replies in the last twelve context messages, no older than twelve hours when timestamped. Daytime and nighttime window wording are distinguished. This is a wording/deduplication policy, not an authorization to confirm a booking or change reception's obligations. Literal prices, dates, questions and caveats about unapproved rates must be retained.
