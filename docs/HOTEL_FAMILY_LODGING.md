# Family lodging intake

Guest count alone no longer routes an ordinary lodging question to reception as a group/event quotation. Family stays needing several rooms remain eligible for AI intake. Ambiguous adult counts and room distribution must be clarified before estimating; known dates and child ages must not be requested again.

Published room capacities and approved rates still constrain recommendations and estimates. The hotel confirms availability and the final price within its existing follow-up window. Negotiated group rates, corporate agreements, excursions/delegations, events, payments and changes to existing quotes/bookings retain their reception routing. New event quotation triage is unchanged.

An already recorded reception handoff is not replayed merely because a later policy update changes classification. Without a newer customer message, dispatch exits before model generation or transport. This preserves existing pending cases and prevents sending a message to Pineda as a side effect of deployment. Manual pauses, staff quiet windows, scheduled attention hours and commercial-followup settings are unchanged.

Regression coverage includes Pineda's literal question, family multi-room lodging, explicit group/corporate pricing, ordinary large headcounts, events and dispatch recovery of an already handed-off request. Tests use mocks and do not contact customers.
